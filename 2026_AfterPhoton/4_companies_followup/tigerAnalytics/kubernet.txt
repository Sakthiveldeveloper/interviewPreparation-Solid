# 🧠 2. THEORY QUESTIONS

TOP KUBERNETES INTERVIEW QUESTIONS

1. What is Kubernetes?
   Kubernetes is a container orchestration platform to manage and scale containerized applications.

---

2. What is a Pod?
   Smallest deployable unit containing one or more containers.

---

3. What is a Cluster?
   Group of nodes running Kubernetes.

---

4. What is Deployment?
   Manages pods and ensures desired state.

---

5. What is Service?
   Exposes pods to network.

---

6. Types of Services?

* ClusterIP
* NodePort
* LoadBalancer

---

7. What is ConfigMap?
   Stores configuration data.

---

8. What is Secret?
   Stores sensitive data securely.

---

9. What is HPA?
   Auto scales pods based on load.

---

10. What is Ingress?
    Manages external access to services.

---

11. What is Rolling Update?
    Update pods gradually without downtime.

---

12. What is Self-healing?
    Automatically restarts failed pods.

---

13. What is Namespace?
    Logical separation inside cluster.

---

14. What is Helm?
    Package manager for Kubernetes.

---

15. Docker vs Kubernetes?
    Docker = container runtime
    Kubernetes = orchestration
